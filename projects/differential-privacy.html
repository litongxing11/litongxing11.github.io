PRIVACY & DATA ANALYSIS

Local or central? Studying differential privacy.

I investigated when local differential privacy might be preferable to central differential privacy for measuring online advertising campaigns.

When
Grades 11–12
Context
Independent research
My role
Researcher

The question

How do privacy protection and the usefulness of released statistics change under local and central differential privacy? I explored the question in a synthetic advertising setting, varying privacy budgets, dataset sizes, and click rates.

What I did

  • Simulated 10,000 users and more than 88,000 advertisement impressions across eight campaigns.
  • Implemented and evaluated three differential privacy mechanisms in Python.
  • Compared accuracy, campaign ranking, and attribute-inference privacy risks across experimental conditions.

Research output

I wrote an 8,000+ word research paper, published in the National High School Journal of Science. My mentor for this work was Professor Andrew Blumberg of Columbia University.
