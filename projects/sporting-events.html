MATHEMATICAL MODELING & SUSTAINABILITY

Measuring the footprint of major sporting events.

I led a two-person team in the High School Mathematical Contest in Modeling to evaluate the environmental footprint of major sporting events.

When
Grade 11
Context
HiMCM 2025
My role
Two-person team leader

The question

How can host cities be compared on sustainability when an event affects energy use, greenhouse gas emissions, water consumption, and waste generation?

Our approach

  • Developed hierarchical sub-models for the environmental indicators.
  • Applied an entropy/TOPSIS framework to rank host cities by sustainability performance.
  • Used Super Bowl LIX data to validate the model and extended the framework to multi-stage international tournaments, including the BWF World Tour.

My role & output

I led the two-person modeling team. Our work resulted in a 25-page research report; HiMCM 2025 recognized the team with an Honorable Mention.
