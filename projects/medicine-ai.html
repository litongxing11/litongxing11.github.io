WORKING iOS PROTOTYPE

MedicineAI: medication routines, care notes, and accessible AI explanations.

Originally developed for the Conrad Challenge, MedicineAI has evolved into a native iPhone app prototype designed for older adults and people managing chronic conditions. It brings medication reminders, saved notes, label photos, and optional AI explanations into one interface, with an Easy Mode that offers larger controls and voice features.

The goal

Bring medication routines and appointment notes into a clear, accessible interface. MedicineAI lets users photograph printed labels or notes, review the extracted text, set their own reminder schedules, and request explanations of selected note text. The design keeps users in control of what they save, record, and share.

Two workflows, one app.

What I built

Notes and label-text recognition

Photograph visit notes or printed medication labels, extract text with Apple Vision, and correct it against the original image before saving.

Flexible medication routines

Enter medication names, instructions, and times. Repeat schedules can cover every day, weekdays, weekends, or custom weekdays, and can be paused, resumed, or archived.

Reference photos and intake history

Notifications use generic wording. Opening a reminder shows the saved medication and label photo inside the app. Taken, Skipped, and Undo controls record the user's own actions.

Reviewed AI explanations

Select note text, prepare a question, and review exactly what will be shared. Optional DeepSeek explanations use an authenticated backend that keeps provider credentials out of the app.

Easy Mode and voice interaction

A simplified interface offers larger controls and clearer steps. On supported devices, users can speak a question, correct the transcript, and explicitly apply it. Answers have user-controlled read-aloud and Stop actions.

Four languages and local storage

The interface supports English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, and French. Saved notes, photos, schedules, and intake history use protected local storage; signing in does not automatically upload them.

Project history

I began MedicineAI as a Grade 10 Conrad Challenge project and was a finalist in the China Conrad Challenge. I have continued developing it into a native iPhone prototype, expanding the work from its original concept into medication scheduling, reviewed text recognition, accessible interaction, and a controlled AI backend.

The current version is installed on an iPhone for private testing. Development has focused on the complete workflow: entering information, checking extracted text, opening reminders, preserving drafts, and handling permissions and interruptions.

Methods & tools

Swift · SwiftUI · Apple Vision OCR · Apple Speech · AVFoundation · UserNotifications · Supabase · PostgreSQL · DeepSeek API

Testing & current status

As of October 28, 2026, MedicineAI is a working iOS prototype in private testing. The latest recorded iOS suite executed 285 tests, with two expected device-only skips and no failures; the backend suite passed 305 tests.

Physical-iPhone checks have covered camera capture, reviewed text recognition, saved-photo reminders, spoken questions, and audio playback. Testing uses fictional data and focuses on software behavior, privacy boundaries, accessibility, and failure handling. The app has not been publicly released on the App Store.

Scope

MedicineAI supports organizing information and requesting explanations of selected text. It does not identify pills by appearance, choose medication doses or schedules, diagnose conditions, or replace advice from a clinician or pharmacist.
