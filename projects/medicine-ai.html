Medicine AI: a medication-assistance prototype.

For the Conrad Challenge, I developed an AI-powered medication-assistance prototype for older adults and people with chronic diseases.

When
Grade 10
Context
Conrad Challenge
My role
Student developer

The goal

Bring information from medical records, medication reminders, and daily reports into an interface designed for users and caregivers.

What I built

  • Integrated optical character recognition and TextCNN to process medical records.
  • Designed medication reminders, pill-image alerts, and daily health reports.
  • Developed the user interface with PyQt5 and tested the prototype using medical data.

Project context

The work was developed for the Conrad Challenge. I was a China Conrad Challenge finalist in Grade 10.

Methods & tools

OCR · TextCNN · Python · PyQt5
