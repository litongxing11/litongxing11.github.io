FORECASTING & ENVIRONMENTAL MODELING

The environmental cost of computing.

I led a two-person quantitative modeling project on the environmental impact of high-performance computing, focusing on global data centers.

When
Grade 10
Context
Quantitative modeling project
My role
Two-person team leader

The question

How can we assess and forecast computing infrastructure’s energy consumption, carbon emissions, and water usage?

Our approach

  • Analyzed the environmental footprint of global data centers.
  • Developed ARIMA and LSTM models to forecast future trends.
  • Used the modeling work to propose sustainable solutions.

My role & output

I led the two-person team and wrote a research report of more than 20 pages.

Methods & tools

ARIMA · LSTM · Energy, carbon & water modeling
